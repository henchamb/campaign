## The Tavern

The campaign opens with each player keeping to themselves in separate corners of a tavern in Nightstone called "The Dreaming Boar." The lord and lawgiver of this settlement is named Lady Velrosa Nandar. It was a rowdy night at the inn, with several groups of so-called adventurers making plans to capitalize on the lucrative work rumored to be posted in the morning.

The coming work isn't the only thing being discussed in the tavern. Many other rumors swirl about the place:

> [!whisper]+ Tavern Rumors
> - The Lord's Alliance has withdrawn its standing guard from the surrounding area and is consolidating them in Waterdeep. This has left the smaller towns and caravans on the Trade Way vulnerable to attack by goblins, bandits, and the like.
> - There are rumors of great unrest in the city of Waterdeep. You've heard many second hand accounts of violence involving multiple criminal factions.
> - It's difficult to enter Waterdeep currently. You've heard rumors of long lines, high tariffs, and interrogations at the gates. There is a small city of caravans and tents popped up outside the city's moat. Traders, entertainers, and travelers occupy the area trying to wait out the troubled times.
> - Crime and other dark activity is on the rise on the Sword Coast. Posted signs in Nightstone and on the Trade Way warn of roaming goblins, occult related kidnappings and murders, and more.

The adventure begins with a lone Drow named Pip being accosted by drunk bandits while sitting at the bar. The bandits took issue with her heritage and demanded she leave, or else. Finally fed up with their behavior, a striking woman with dark skin and silver hair (Esme), summoned a mage hand to throw the bandit's drink into his face. This leads to both Esme and Pip being seized by the bandits, who attempt to drag both ladies outside the establishment. Before they make it far, there is a flash of movement from above as a tall man with dark hair (Jockamo) drops from the second story of the tavern and lands on one of the bandits. The man then quickly draws a dagger and holds it to the bandit's throat. Terse words are exchanged, and the now terrified bandits are convinced to leave the place quickly and quietly.

Pip, Esme, and Jockamo share drinks and conversation at the tavern. They notice that a halfling woman (Vaama) is paying close attention. Vaama, who seems to have many friends around the place, joins their conversation. The four of them resolve to join forces and try to find mercenary work in the morning.

Outside the bar they pass by an Elf quietly strumming a stringed instrument on a bench (Marok). The Elf is cryptic and vague, drawing the suspicion of other members of the group. They part ways after a short conversation.

Vaama is able to use her connection with Norm, the owner of The Dreaming Boar, to secure two rooms at the inn for free. Jockamo briefly steps outside, telling the party he is leaving to offer a prayer to his god. He approaches the drawbridge and drops down the ravine next to the riverbed. He notices he is being followed by Marok, and the two have a tense conversation before Jockamo returns to the tavern.

## The Morning Contract

In the morning, the group finds the job board. They claim the contract to deal with goblin activity in the area and are referred to the local constable, Frieda Greatheart. Frieda doesn't think that the goblins are a big threat and thinks that the town would be better off if people focused on more important matters. This doesn't dissuade the group, however, and Frieda refers the group to a local game hunter named Dawn Mistwalker.

> [!flavor]+ From Jockamo
> "As the group crossed town from Frieda to Dawn, Marok was visible on a roof. Jockamo brought up in conversation that Marok was watching them and that Marok had followed Jockamo the night before as Jockamo went to pay homage to his God. Jockamo shared that he didn't think Marok has ill intentions, but he's certainly not offering information on whatever threat he keeps alluding to. Esme mind-talks to Marok asking 'What are you doing here?' and Marok responds out loud, 'I see you made it through the night' generally to Jockamo. The group accepts the ominousness, but stays on course to Dawn. Dawn cracks the door open after they knock and responds with light distaste when they name drop Frieda, but starts to engage in regards to the contract. Marok drops down from the roof to eavesdrop, but is heard by Pip and Jockamo. Dawn notices their reaction to Marok's presence and (displeased with the surprise extra visitor) shuts the door on them. Jockamo is pissed and tells the group he's gonna beat Marok up for foiling the contract if no one has a peaceful way to demand why he's following us. Jockamo gets Marok in a headlock, knife to throat. Esme gives him a smorg for SOME reason lol and then Pip beats him up. Vama keeps him from dying, but he's now unconscious."
