## The Aftermath in Nightstone

The session opens with Marok lying unconscious in Jockamo's arms. Pip, not knowing if the strange elf is alive or dead, flees immediately. The guards attracted by the disturbance follow after Pip instead of heading towards the party.

After rifling through his belongings and stealing a flask of holy water (Esme), the group stands by as Vaama places her hands on the unconscious Marok and heals him back to health. Marok is briefly questioned to no avail, and slips away as soon as he can.

Esme and Jockamo hide from the thugs that come to the butcher shop, while Vaama waits patiently for her turn to speak to the butcher's wife. The wife, Lia, tells Vaama to knock on the side door of the butcher's property and ask for Dawn. Dawn knows of the goblins and offers to show the party to their hideout, but she refuses to aid them in their task of "fully resolving the issue." She mentions her disdain of the Lord's Alliance in her refusal.

Dawn also turns out to be more than just a hunter. Her main interest is uncovering the hidden secrets left behind by the ancient Moon Elf Society of Ardeep. She does not recall the time when the great elf societies ruled the Sword Coast, but she does recall her friendship with several of the ancient Moon Elves that lingered before their final departure in 1344 DR (150 yrs ago). The Ardeep forest used to span across the sword coast "from the Golden Hills to the Sword Mountains," before recent human logging and agricultural interests (within the last 150 years) decimated the forest to what exists today.

## The Goblin Hideout

The party follows the game trail successfully, noting a fresh set of humanoid and booted footprints moving in their same direction. They lost track of the humanoid trail somewhat outside of the goblin hideout.

The group sneaks up to the goblin hideout, and then takes quite a long time discussing what to do. During this lively discussion, a goblin patrol is able to sneak up on them and surprise them with a volley of arrows. The party fights the goblins, killing 2 of 3, but letting one escape down the stairs. The group then decides to hide a distance away from the hideout.

Several minutes later, they watch around a dozen goblins come out of the cave ready for a fight. They search the immediate area, then form 3 groups of 4 and begin to patrol in different areas. The patrol that passes close by the party does not notice them.

As the patrols pass by, the party decides that now is the perfect time to enter the hideout and plunder to their hearts content. They also determine that they can catch their enemy unaware if they sneak in successfully. They enter the hideout, and discover a foyer flanked by two identical statues. A slender woman in robes, wearing a helmet that completely covers her face except for her mouth, which is curled into a slight smirk. The inscription beneath each statue reads (in Elvish) "Blessed be Her Divine Absence."

Rounding the corner, you come across a mostly abandoned Goblin barracks. Jockamo quickly dispatches the one remaining Goblin with his knife. Vaama keeps watch towards the entrance while this is happening.

## The Zombies

The adjacent room is staked shut with roughshod iron rods that have been driven into the stone floor. After attempting to remove the stakes from the ground (making a slight ruckus), the party perceives the guttural moaning of multiple voices through the door. Mistaking this sound for a prisoner or helpless animal, the party attempts to communicate. This attention further provokes the 4 zombies that are behind that door. The zombies successfully break down the door and a battle ensues. Vaama quickly joins from around the corner and absolutely roasts a zombie. Esme falls unconscious trying to escape from multiple undead foes.

After the battle, Vaama heard the faint pitter patter of little goblin feet running overhead above ground. Time was short. The party decides to hide the evidence of their violence, push a book shelf in front of the old door, and trust in their perception that the goblins are terrified of this room. On the east-facing wall, they notice a mural of what looks to be the Nightstone castle, except there is a gigantic obsidian obelisk standing in the center of the main square. That obelisk is nowhere to be found in the present time.

They rest successfully, although the night is fraught with the sounds of returning goblin patrols. They awaken at some unknown hour of the early morning, and the day is theirs.

Where is Marok during all this? The party has no idea. Little do they know that he is only 30 minutes away from discovering the goblin hideout at this very moment.
