# Session 4

> [!nav]
> [[Dashboard]] · Prev: [[Session 3]] · Next: [[Session 5]]

## Chaos in the Temple

The session opens in a moment of chaos. Jockamo holds a terrified goblin by the neck, Esme is torn apart by the recently deceased worgs and lying unconscious, and a familiar elf has reappeared. The party briefly interrogates the goblin creature, who is then hogtied and ballgagged. Vaama, always vigilant, steps into the dark stone hallway to listen for any activity, and she hears the sounds of wooden objects being dragged across the stone floor in the distance. The party correctly deduces that the goblins are building a barrier. Their goblin captive reveals that they are most likely holed up in the "dining hall" further into the temple.

Meanwhile, Dawn Mistwalker, who was with Marok when he charged into the temple to join the party in combat, decides to follow her curiosity and creeps down the steps into the temple. She is deeply unsettled as she approaches the twin statues of a figure she recognizes–the evil and powerful goddess Shar. She finds the party mid-discussion, and shares what information she knows about the goddess, as well as issues a word of warning: There might be more danger here than a goblin tribe. Despite the party's persuasion attempts, she still will not slay any creature for the Lord's Alliance. Sensing greater danger, she does, however, gift the party a Potion of Healing (2d4+2) as she wishes them luck. Jockamo executes the hogtied goblin.

## Pip's Journey

Pip begins her day around 4am, standing on the Trade Way. She wants to find her new friends, and she knows they were sent to kill goblins deep in the Ardeep Forest. Scanning for any potentially helpful individuals, she notices a hunter making his way into the trees. She hails the hunter, who turns out to be a pleasant but gruff older man, and learns that this man knows Dawn pretty well–himself being a veteran explorer of the woods. In fact, Dawn has shown him the game trail that the goblins use to get in and out of town. He agrees to forgo his morning hunt and act as Pip's guide for 5 silver. They begin their journey toward the temple.

## The Altar of Shar

Deep inside the forest, the party makes a plan to assault the goblin cafeteria. First, they spend a minute searching a nearby storage room, but find nothing of interest. Now spreading the blood and viscera of the executed goblin on their faces and bodies, they do a spectacular zombie walk down the dark hallway into the next chamber. They discover, however, that this room is not the dining hall, but a vaulted chamber with stone pews organized to face an altar displaying the symbol of Shar. Behind the altar, a 30 foot tall stone statue of Shar herself, depicted as she usually is with her iron cowl covering all her face save her mouth, which is curled into a subtle, smirking smile. A search of the room reveals that the statue is protected all around by some sort of invisible barrier. They also find a conspicuous looking "slot" in the floor behind the altar. They conclude that something definitely fits in there. The party also notices what looks to be a donation box, currently protected by the force field. Anxious to keep the initiative in their fight with the goblin tribe, they press on quickly.

The next hallway reveals the source of the scraping noises. The goblins have erected a 6ft tall barricade made from broken wood, whole furniture, loose stones, and trash. After some acrobatic scouting, the party notices what might be another way into the dining hall. Esme remains at the barricade to keep watch while Jockamo, Marok, and Vaama split off down the opposite hallway looking for another route. They enter a round chamber with a pool of water in the center. Obscuring their view is dense fog throughout the room. Noticing more obsidian statues built into reliefs in the walls, the party quietly makes their way through the room. They unlock the door on the opposite end and creep down the next hallway. After a close call with a goblin coming from deeper in the temple, Jockamo does a solo scouting mission and finds what looks like the goblin's collective bathroom, just a pile of shit in the middle of a small room. They find the back entrance to the dining hall, and initiate combat with the 7 goblins in the room (earning a surprise round through their stealth).

## Ralli and the Lady

The party is victorious against the goblins in the dining hall, but uneased that one of their foes had tried to run further into the temple for help. Clutching yet another goblin hostage, the party tries to get more information. Before their interrogation can begin, however, they are interrupted by a small voice coming from the kitchen. Poking her head out of the door is a gnome who introduces herself as Ralli. She had been taken hostage by the goblins days ago, and made to work in the kitchen. She has absolutely no love for the goblins and savagely murders your hostage with an iron cooking pot. Before the murder takes place, the party learns about the final occupants of this temple. Two hobgoblins and "the lady," whom the goblins greatly fear and pay tribute to. The goblins are terrified of Shar and believe the Lady to be her vessel or some type of important servant. Ralli can confirm  the existence of the hobgoblins and the Lady, though she has never seen her. After a frantic search for her "horn," she pulls a unique looking instrument from a garbage pile. An instrument of brass adorned with various buttons and valves. The party (the characters themselves) have never seen anything remotely like this instrument.

At about this time, Pip and her hired guide arrive at the entrance to the Sharran temple. Pip enters the temple and begins to retrace the steps of the party. Finding herself in the storage room, she finds something the party missed–an obsidian disk with the symbol of Shar engraved on it. Hugging this large piece of stone, she casually makes her way to the barricade. Hearing the familiar voices of the party on the other side, she eavesdrops a bit before clambering over the barrier to join the group. She's clutching the key to the force field maybe! The party inserts the disk into the slot and the barrier goes down. They bust open the donation box and find some gold.

## The Final Door

The party fears the unknown powers of the Lady, but are determined to see their contract through. If there are two hobgoblins alive in this temple, then their job is not yet complete. Of the two remaining rooms left unexplored in this ancient complex, Ralli is certain that the hobgoblins reside in the left one. A brief investigation shows evidence of hobgoblin habitation, but the left room is currently vacant. The party concludes that the remaining two enemies have rallied around the Lady to provide protection. They create an escape plan by stacking alcohol soaked straw mattresses in a pile in the hallway, intending to set them alight to cover their escape. During this process, the ever perceptive Vaama hears the sound of a female voice reciting some type of incantation–yet the voice seems to be coming from below them.

With Jockamo in the lead, the party quietly approaches the final door. As they round the corner, several things happen. First, the party's ranger is caught uncharacteristically unaware by a figure waiting in the doorway. The figure, holding a longbow, releases the arrow it had at the ready and hits Jockamo squarely in the heart. He crumples to the ground immediately.

Then, darkness. Not the usual darkness of the temple, but a terrible, magical darkness that creates a complete void of sight for anyone within it. Even the party members with darkvision were blinded by this spell. The party then fights desperately in the confusion as they try to escape down the hall. Esme drags Jockamo's body down the hallway while Marok and Pip attack blindly at the hobgoblins, who rush forward with seemingly no regard for their own survival. The party notices the blank expression of the creatures, almost as if they were under some sort of bewitchment. The well armored hobgoblins are tough to hit, but the party prevails against them. Jockamo is brought back to life by Vaama, who uses the last of her energy to heal his wounds.

As the party catches its breath just outside the radius of the magical darkness, they watch as the spell begins to recede. Slowly, the light in the hallway in front of them returns to normal. Some members of the party notice a distant rumbling, intense but far away.

## The Mask

After some discussion, the party decides to face their fears and check out the final chamber. As they enter, they find a comfortable room with a real bed, a desk, chest, and bookshelf. The chest is easily unlocked by the deft hand of Jockamo, but as he opens the container he is pierced by a small poisoned dart shooting from a hidden trap. The poison proves too much for the beleaguered Jockamo to handle, and he once again falls unconscious. The party unceremoniously reaches over Jockamo's still body to retrieve the gold in the chest before he is stabilized by Vaama.

There is an open hatch leading downward in the room. The party is split on whether to go down it or not, but eventually Esme and Vaama descend. They find the lifeless body of a familiar looking figure in the center of a round chamber, the floor of which is taken up entirely by an arcane sigil or some kind. They can't quite figure out who this person is, or how she is familiar. She is wearing a full face mask made of obsidian. Esme and Vaama search the body, finding a journal, but do not dare remove the mask.

Satisfied and exhausted, the party drags the limp Jockamo out of the temple and into the filtered sunshine of the forest clearing. As they (accompanied by Ralli) emerge from the mouth of the subterranean temple, they reach level 2!!! Vaama uses her newfound strength to dig deep and cast one more healing spell on poor Jockamo, who returns to life. The session ends as the party is contemplating a well earned rest.
