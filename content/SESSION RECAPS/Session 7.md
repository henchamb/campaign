# Session 7

## The Contract

The session begins several minutes after the slaying of the insect-like monstrosity that erupted suddenly from the ground. The previously crowded main square of Nightstone is now abandoned except for a few brave and curious onlookers. The sounds of disturbances echo around town as the city guard and other mercenaries deal with the remaining giant rats.

Jockamo shares a mysterious mercenary contract with the group, and the players–with some hesitation–decide to help him with this job in hopes of securing proper credentials to enter the city of [[Waterdeep]]. Jockamo shares the goals of the contract with the party: assassinate a figure known as [[Regan Thomas]] and find out who he has been working for. Regan Thomas is currently in custody of the Nightstone guard somewhere, and there is a [[Lord's Alliance]] escort en route to collect him tomorrow morning. After the prisoner changes hands it will be too late to get to him.

To accomplish these goals, the party ponders possible tools such as poison, acid, and truth serum. Vaama and Esme use their knowledge of nature, arcana, and alchemy to provide details as to the availability/possibility of these different options. The players decide to return to [[Tom & Len]], and possibly pay another visit to the [[Archmage Daneel]].

## Gathering Information

To the party's surprise, Daneel is, in fact, currently present in the main square of Nightstone. He stands above the party atop the massive pile of rubble, and seems to be studying the location of the seismic anomaly. The party goes to speak with him, and he shares his initial thoughts on what he thinks occurred in the town square. He provides some insight on the viability of creating a truth serum, saying that it would be expensive and time consuming to create a custom brew such as this. He tells the party that he may have some single-use scrolls that could help, and to visit his tower if they are interested.

After parting ways with the mysterious wizard, the party heads back out to the WSC camp to have a chat with Tom & Len. There they learn the location of their target–a guard garrison on the southern island of Nightstone. They also learn that one of their own, Pip, was responsible for bringing this person into custody in Nightstone. Tom & Len point the party towards the victims of Regan who had been traveling with him before they were kidnapped.

In search of further insight into their target, the party decides to talk to some of the victims. As they approach, a young girl with curly bright  red hair runs up to Pip and embraces her. The [[Sierra, Penny, & Thomas Gander|Ganders]] (Sierra, Penny, and Thomas) thank Pip again for rescuing them, and **they invite Pip and her friends to visit and stay with Penny's cousin, [[Blossom Snobeedle]], at the [[Snobeedle Orchard and Meadery|Snobeedle Orchards and Meadery]] outside of the city walls.** They are eager to aid the party, and don't seem bothered when it is implied that assassination is the goal. However, their knowledge of Regan is vague and they are unable to provide much insight to help the party with their goals.

## Surveillance & The Plan

The guard garrison imprisoning the party's target is on the smaller, southern island set between the splitting currents of the [[Ardeep River]]. Rising above this island, in addition to the garrison, is Nightstone Keep; the residence of [[Lady Velrosa Nandar|Lady Velrosa]], the lord of Nightstone. The garrison consists of two large stone buildings set on either side of the bridge. The party walks a short distance and begins watching the garrison from the west side of the river, adjacent to a traveling merchant with some horses loosely corralled in the area. Esme attempts to steal a horse but is noticed immediately.

After gathering some information through their surveillance, the party returns to the Dreaming Boar. Seeking inside information, they scan the bar for anyone they might recognize as an off-duty guard. Vaama recognizes one of the Boar's patrons as a guard, and Jockamo approaches for a conversation. As he chats with the guard (Huey), he learns that the shift change is at 6pm, and that another guard (Geoffrey) drew the short straw for watching the prisoner. The party gets the sense that the standing guard of Nightstone does not take their job very seriously–the heavy stuff always being handled by the Lord's Alliance security forces.

The party decides to approach their new friend Huey after shift change.They bring a bottle of booze and offer it as a gift to Geoffrey. Huey accepts the gift and delivers it across the bridge. Esme's fox familiar follows stealthily behind, and slips through the door to enter the stone building where the target is being held. Through her fox's eyes, Esme describes the scene of Geoffrey getting good and drunk. The session ends with the party waiting for the right moment at the Dreaming Boar. (about 7pm, 19th of Marpenoth)
