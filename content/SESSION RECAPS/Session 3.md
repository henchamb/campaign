# Session 3

> [!nav]
> Prev: [[Session 2]] · Next: [[Session 4]]

> [!info] At a Glance
> **Location:** Sharran temple, [[Ardeep Forest]] · **Level:** 1

> [!note]+ Addendum to Session 2
> The medallion that you found in Marok's pack represents the undead dragon-god [[Icasaracht]]. With a successful history check, Esme recalled a few details about this evil deity. Icasaracht hated humankind intensely and sought to wipe out the "new" races, returning dominion over the world to the ancients. He was dead and gone from this world for over a thousand years before he was eventually resurrected by his cult following. His second life was short-lived, however, as he was killed shortly after his resurrection.

## Scaring the Goblins

We rejoin our heroes as they come to consciousness in the early morning. After a quick look around the room and some strategizing, the players decide to give the goblins a good scare. Esme and Vaama throw down the bookshelf blocking the passageway with a loud crash, and Jockamo, being covered in blood and viscera, does his best zombie impersonation as he exits the room. This terrifies the goblins, and Jockamo watches as most of them retreat, leaving 4 behind. There is a quick combat in the bunkroom. Three goblins are defeated, one runs away down the opposite hallway. Following the goblin's trail, the party comes to a room where two worgs are being kept. They defeat the worgs with the help of a familiar figure. The party is surprised to see Marok has followed them to this ancient underground lair.

## Marok's Path

Unknown to the rest of the party, Marok had an encounter with [[Dawn Mistwalker]], who recognized the same elf that was following them outside [[Nightstone]]. She was suspicious, but Marok eventually convinced her that he wanted to help. She told Marok that she may have found another entrance to the underground chambers. They were about to head that way when Marok heard the sounds of combat coming from the mouth of the main entrance. He heads straight in and joins the fray. We end the session just as combat ends. The party has not yet spoken to or greeted their new arrival, and there is still a goblin hiding somewhere around. Esme is unconscious again after getting torn up by the two worgs.

Level 1 characters are fragile!

> [!note]+ Important DM Note
> Esme technically died during the worg combat, but I decided to keep her alive. We are all still learning, especially me as DM, so this first dungeon is kind of like our tutorial to the campaign. I don't want any permanent character death to happen while we're all still figuring things out. Once we get further into the game, however, character death absolutely can happen–so be prepared for that!
