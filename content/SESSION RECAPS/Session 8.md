# Session 8

> [!nav]
> Prev: [[Session 7]] · Next: [[Session 9]]

> [!info] At a Glance
> **Location:** [[Nightstone]] — [[The Dreaming Boar|Dreaming Boar]], main square, guard garrison · **Level:** 2

## Daneel's Package & Dawn's Warning

The session begins in the Dreaming Boar. The party is nervously awaiting the nightfall. [[Norm Amberway|Norm]] approaches the party with a small package, neatly wrapped in brown paper. He tells the party that it was left by the [[Archmage Daneel]]. The package contained a potion of detect thoughts, as well as a note from the mage.

A little later, [[Dawn Mistwalker]] enters the inn looking for the party. She finds them and immediately shares what she discovered during her research in the ancient temple's library— the mask's enchantment cannot be broken using the normal methods. Attempting to dispel the mask will only temporarily subdue the connection to the goddess [[Shar]]. Dawn tells the party that the mask is still dangerous and its power could return at any moment.

## Pip and the Shadow Demon

Alarmed at this information, the party rushes into Pip's room, as she was the one carrying the mask. Entering the room, they discover that both Pip and the mask are gone. Only a letter remains. The party reads the letter (the one you wrote), and quickly assume the worst. Pip has clearly been taken under the influence of the mask.

As they are processing this information, they hear the sounds of shouting outside in the main square:

> [!flavor]+ Outside the Dreaming Boar
> "Look! It's a drow! She's casting a spell!"

The party rushes outside the inn. There they see Pip, but she's wearing the mask of Shar. She floats above the mountain of rubble in the main square as dark shadow swirls around her.

Before the party can react, a creature seeps forth from the depths, moving through the stone and earth like a ghost. The creature looms tall and terrible above the rubble—taking the form of a demon dripping with dark shadows. The shadow demon turns its head towards the party, and they feel the overwhelming sensation of the void. All of their thoughts, feelings, and senses start to unravel as together they succumb to the overwhelming nothingness.

The demon, encouraged by its domination of the party, moves to strike against these would-be heroes that dare to oppose the will of Shar; and it would have taken them all.

But in that moment of hopelessness, a hidden power reveals itself. In comes a giant dragon, beautiful with shining blue scales yet still terrible to behold. The dragon unleashes a roar that shakes the earth, and as it roars it unleashes a beam of light that smites the shadow demon. As the dragon attacks, the party hears a voice echo inside of their heads—Dawn's voice:

> [!flavor]+ Dawn's Voice
> "Begone, foul creature. There is life here still and you are unwelcome. Return now to the void from whence you came."

Dismayed by the strength of the dragon, the shadow demon retreats. As it returns to the earth, it wraps its arms around Pip, and together the two of them sink deep underground.

The party returns to Pip's room and has a good look around. While they look, Dawn explains that she is a Spirit Dragon, and that her lifeforce is connected to the ancient magic in the [[Ardeep Forest|Ardeep forest]] blah blah blah…

## The Assassination

Jockamo and Marok decide to take advantage of the confusion. They notice that the gate guards are out of position, and sneak across the bridge with Esme's fox-familiar Mermin in tow. Esme's external senses go dead as she begins to see and hear through the eyes and ears of her companion. Vaama keeps careful watch on the situation.

Looking through the window, the Jock-arok (or Marokamo) see that the guard on duty is fast asleep, snoring lightly and cradling a mostly drunk bottle. In the cell, curled up on the stone floor facing the corner, they see a figure that they assume to be their target. They enter the room carefully, waking neither of the room's occupants. Marok, who had quickly removed his armor before the infiltration, takes position behind the sleeping guard. Jockamo crouches for a small stone, and throws it quietly to wake the prisoner. Having quickly taken the potion of Detect Thoughts, he feels the effect take hold on [[Regan Thomas|Regan]] (*side note: i wonder what he would have seen if he used the potion on him while he was sleeping?*). Jockamo fools Regan into thinking that he represents his current employers, learning a little about the organization in the process.  He sees a red skinned tiefling that has been Regans's point of contact–although the tiefling's name and affiliation remain unknown. He also sees images of the drop point, including flashes of memory involving Pip & Co's assault on the location.

Regan is unlocked and goes to fetch his gear from a nearby chest, at that point the assassination begins. Marok quickly knocks out the guard with a blow. Regan is savvy and battle hardened, he doesn't go down immediately. He attempts to escape out the doors, getting partially up the wall before an arrow from Jockamo finally finishes the job. He falls with a thud.

Vaama is keeping a close eye on the garrison, and notices the shadowy figures emerge- one running, two giving chase. She confirms that the guards are still unaware of what is in progress. A dull thud echoes from behind the garrison, confused guards leave their post to investigate. Taking advantage of the building as cover, Jockamo and Marok are safely back across the bridge before the body is discovered.

The party heads straight to establish their alibi with [[Tom & Len]]. "They were drinking and gambling with the [[Wandering Star Collective|WSC]] all night." They do, in fact, do some gambling and Esme takes the pot! As it gets late, Mermin is able to drop a confirmation message to Jockamo's contact. The session ends at 11pm.
