## The Attack at Dawn

Vaama awakes suddenly from a strange dream to find intruders in their cozy room in the Dreaming Boar. Two dark figures, one silently climbing through the window, the other standing over Esme holding a knife.

Combat ensues, as the players fight back. Vaama bangs on the wall to call for help and that wakes up Jockamo and Marok. They join the fight. Also joining the fight is the red skinned tiefling from [[Regan Thomas|Regan]]' s memory, and a devilish imp. The party successfully eliminate the threat, leaving one alive but unconscious. They find a piece of parchment written in undercommon and 4 medals bearing the symbol of Asmodious (a 9 pointed star). At this point [[Norm Amberway|Norm]] enters the room and is stunned and terrified, he asks a few fumbling questions before declaring that he must summon the guard. Not wanting to get caught up in that, the party quickly questions their captive before killing him, and then drops out the window.

## Escape with the WSC

They head straight for WSC, finding the camps to be just beginning to stir. [[Tom & Len]] lead the party to their favorite hiding spot in the caravan, and nestled inside the folds of the performance tents is where we end session 9, at around 6am, Marp 20th. The caravan is just beginning to move out.
