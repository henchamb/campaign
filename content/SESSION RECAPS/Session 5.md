# Session 5

> [!nav]
> [[Dashboard]] · Prev: [[Session 4]] · Next: [[Session 6]]

## The Forest Clearing

The session begins with the party recovering in the forest clearing outside the Sharran temple. Ralli Batterton requests an escort back to Nightstone, and agrees to accompany the party to Godson and verify their success in eliminating the goblin threat. Dawn, having scouted the perimeter, approaches the party and reveals the presence of two boats hidden in the brush next to the river. Dawn asks the party how everything went inside the temple, and was alarmed and concerned by the presence of an unknown being attempting some type of dark ritual. The party accompanies her as she investigates the area. She removes the mask from the corpse in the deepest chamber and reveals Frieda Greatheart, the current constable of Nightstone. She cautiously allows Esme to handle and keep the mask on the condition that they bring it to see Daneel, the mage who lives to the south of Nightstone.

## Return to Nightstone

The party leaves the temple and decides to return to Nightstone by river and then continue south to visit the local wizard Daneel. They safely make the journey, and the players read Frieda's journal along the way. The details of the diary are disturbing and grim, describing a local constable's descent into madness after finding a strange Sharran artifact. Players can refer to their notes for details on the journal!

Arriving in Nightstone, the party finds a major disturbance in the main square. A huge pile of dirt and stone has risen in the middle of town! Approaching some guards, the party learns that this incident happened several hours ago and local authorities are still securing the area. The party is referred to Captain Grayson for any other questions. Continuing on to the castle, the party is shown to Godson's quarters, where he is currently engaged in a tense conversation with Captain Grayson. After some convincing, the party receives a receipt of completed work from Godson, but learns that the Lord's Alliance funds have been stolen. Payment will have to be delayed by a couple days.

Disappointed and suspicious, the party leaves Esme's fox in the room as they leave the area. The fox is able to overhear the conversation between Godson and the Captain, and the party learns details about how much was stolen and what is being done about it.

The party then visits Daneel, who dispels the evil magic in the mask. The conversation is cut short as the party seems to annoy the powerful mage with their demands. The party ends their day in the Dreaming Boar, wondering what the next day will bring.
