# Godson Goodrich
*Half-Elf · Male*

**Appearance:** Long dark hair, almost black. Holds his chin high. Richly adorned uniform, ostentatious. Speaks in a self-important, nasally tone.

## What the Party Knows

### Session 1 — The Contract
The Lord's Alliance emissary in Nightstone, sent to establish a mercenary outpost in the region. The goblin contract at the job board was his — the party claimed it and were eventually referred to Frieda, then Dawn.

### Session 5 — Receipt of Completion
The party returned to the castle to report success. Godson was mid-conversation with Captain Grayson when they arrived — something tense. He received the party with barely concealed disdain, treating the interruption like a minor inconvenience. After some convincing, he issued a receipt of completed work. Payment, he said, would be delayed: the Lord's Alliance funds had been stolen.

Esme left Mermin behind in the room on the way out. Through her telepathic link, she heard everything. Godson was dressing Grayson down for letting it happen on his watch. Suspects: two Baldurian males. Amount stolen: over 3,500 Waterdeep Dragons.

## Last Seen
Nightstone, running the Lord's Alliance operation at the castle.
