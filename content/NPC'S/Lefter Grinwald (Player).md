# Lefter Grinwald
*Dwarf · Male*

**Appearance:** Short white hair, balding on top. Long white beard gathered with a gold ring. Grey eyes. Almost always smoking a pipe. Travels in an impressively large carriage.

## What the Party Knows
A Waterdeep banker by inheritance who has taken to a life of travel on the Sword Coast. When refugees near Nightstone were taken in the night, he offered to pay Pip, Tom, Len, and Enyasil to rescue them — and kept watch on Sierra Gander while they did.

The rescue succeeded. He's heading to Waterdeep to check on his business affairs.

The party has not met him. They know this from Tom and Len.
