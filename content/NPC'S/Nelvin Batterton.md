# Nelvin Batterton
*Gnome · Male · 120 years old*

**Appearance:** Slightly darker green hair than his sister Ralli, green/blue eyes. Gruff and abrupt — always seems like his mind is somewhere else entirely.

## What the Party Knows
[[Ralli Batterton]]'s brother and a member of the WSC 4th troupe. A genius inventor of one-of-a-kind musical instruments — he built the Button Horn (Ralli's) and the accordion (Enya's). He only creates for the most accomplished musicians in the WSC, and it's considered a high honor to play one of his pieces.

The party hasn't spoken to him directly.

## Last Seen
WSC caravan, traveling toward Waterdeep alongside the party.
