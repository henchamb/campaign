# Enyasil Briarthorn
*Human · Female*

**Appearance:** 5'6". Red hair, hazel eyes, fair skin. Goes by *Enya*. Plays a unique bellows instrument called the accordion — invented by [[Nelvin Batterton]].

## What the Party Knows

A bard and member of the WSC 4th troupe. Before the rest of the party arrived in Nightstone, Enya had already been tracking Regan Thomas's group on the Trade Way. She was playing cards with Tom & Len at the WSC camp when Pip arrived looking for the missing refugees. She knew where they'd gone.

She joined Pip, Tom & Len on the rescue mission — fought at the smugglers' camp and used her bardic abilities to charm Regan during the confrontation. Helped return the refugees to Nightstone.

### Session 8
Was performing at the WSC rager when the party arrived to establish their alibi. The party gambled — Esme took the pot.

## Last Seen
WSC caravan, traveling toward Waterdeep alongside the party.
