# Regan Thomas
*Human · Male · Late 30s–early 40s*

**Appearance:** Bald. Brown eyes, fair skin, muscular build. Scars on his head, neck, and arms. Battle-hardened — it shows.

## What the Party Knows

Ran a fake refugee escort service on the Trade Way — taking on vulnerable people traveling from Baldur's Gate to Waterdeep and disappearing them along the way. Pip, Tom, Len, and Enya raided his camp and took him captive. He was marched back to Nightstone, said very little, and handed to the guard. The Lord's Alliance had an escort due to collect him the following morning.

**Session 7:** Jockamo brought the party a contract — kill Regan Thomas and find out who he works for, before the Lord's Alliance takes custody. The party scoped the garrison on the southern island. They sent a bottle of booze across the bridge for the guard on duty (Geoffrey), who got good and drunk. Esme slipped Mermin through the door to confirm: Geoffrey out, Regan curled up on the stone floor, facing the corner.

**Session 8 — The Assassination:** With the garrison guards out of position after the chaos in the square, Jockamo and Marok slipped across the bridge. Jockamo woke Regan with a tossed pebble and — using the Detect Thoughts scroll — posed as a representative of his employers. He saw a red-skinned tiefling as Regan's point of contact. He saw flashes of a drop location somewhere off the Trade Way. He saw memories of Pip's assault on the camp.

Regan was unlocked to get his gear. That's when it started. He didn't go quietly — got halfway up the wall before Jockamo's arrow put him down.

The party spent the rest of the night gambling at the WSC rager. Alibi established.

**Session 9 — Aftermath:** The red-skinned tiefling from Regan's memory showed up at the Boar that night with an imp. The party fought them off, kept the tiefling alive for questioning. He gave nothing up — except one slip: *"Asmodeus protect me"* under pressure. That connected him to the nine-pointed star medallions found on the bodies. The party killed him and dropped out the window.

**Open thread:** Jockamo saw flashes of a drop location — a partially collapsed hunting lodge somewhere off the Trade Way. The caravan is heading in that direction.

## Status
**Deceased.** Assassinated Session 8.
