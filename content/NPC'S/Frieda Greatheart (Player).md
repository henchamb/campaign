# Frieda Greatheart
*Half-Orc · Female*

**Appearance:** Light green skin, short slender tusks, dark brown eyes, braided dark brown hair. Was the constable of Nightstone.

---

## What the Party Knows

### Session 1 — First Contact
Frieda was the civilian constable of Nightstone — the official leader of the local guard, ranking above Captain Grayson. When the party picked up the goblin contract at the job board, they were referred to her first. She tried to talk them out of it. The goblins weren't a real threat. There were more important things to worry about. When they didn't budge, she pointed them to Dawn and let them go.

### Session 4 — The Temple
As the party made their final approach inside the temple, a figure waited in the doorway. An arrow dropped Jockamo. Then: total magical darkness that blinded even darkvision. The hobgoblins rushed forward with blank expressions, bewitched. Deep below the floor, a distant rumble.

The darkness eventually receded. Down a hatch in the final room: a round chamber, the floor covered by an arcane sigil, a body in the center wearing a stone mask.

### Session 5 — Revealed
Back in the clearing, Dawn descended into the chamber and removed the mask. The face underneath was Frieda Greatheart.

Esme took the mask on one condition: it goes to Daneel.

**The Journal:** On the boat back to Nightstone, Pip read Frieda's journal aloud — Undercommon sections included.

The journal starts as a mundane read. The thoughts of a modest lawkeeper in a small but troublesome town.

Then: a hunting trip in the Ardeep. Frieda felt watched. She turned and made eye contact with the void-like black eye of a stone mask, partially buried in the earth.

After that, everything changed. She felt "better, powerful, in control" with it on. A pull deeper into the forest — the mask willing her toward something. She followed it to the Temple of the Nightbringers. The source of the mask's power: Shar.

From that point, the journal is two voices.

*Old Frieda (Common):* She tried to destroy the mask. Tried to discard it. Woke up each time with it back in her hands. The mask cannot be destroyed. Exhaustion. Lost time. Blackouts. And then the horror of finding entries written in a language she cannot read: Undercommon.

*Evil Frieda (Undercommon):* A different person entirely. The Nightbringers, she writes, are a sect of Shar worshippers dedicated to expanding the Shadow Weave — Shar's direct challenge to Mystra's Weave. She describes being guided toward "reactivating the beacon": an ancient obelisk buried beneath Nightstone's main square. Activated, it would project the Shadow Weave and magical darkness outward in all directions from Nightstone. Her entries include frenzied drawings of a massive underground structure, miles beneath the town. As for the goblins: she despises them. She calls them "useful slaves" and tolerates them because they're afraid of her and keep others away.

The late entries are entirely in Undercommon. Old Frieda has stopped writing. The new voice talks about keeping up appearances in town, goblins bringing offerings out of fear, and new powers developing. Activating the beacon, she writes, would make her "unstoppable."

The final entry: *"I have arrived and warned the goblin scum about their prey. If we are breached I must attempt the ritual in my current state. If my power is not enough then I will gladly give myself to the void. Our lady will ensure that the work is continued."*

**The Rubble:** Arriving back in Nightstone, the main square was torn open. A massive pile of dirt and stone had pushed up from below — local guards had cordoned it off. The mural in the temple showed a giant obsidian obelisk standing in that square. It's been gone for as long as anyone in town can remember. The magical darkness during the fight, the deep rumbling underfoot, the drawings in the journal, the final entry — they all point to the same moment. The ritual triggered the obelisk to push up through the earth. It failed to breach the surface. The obelisk is still down there.
