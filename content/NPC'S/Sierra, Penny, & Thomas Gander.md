# Sierra, Penny, & Thomas Gander
*Human family from Baldur's Gate*

**Appearance:**
- **Penny** — Red hair, green eyes, female, 40s. Notably short (4'5").
- **Thomas** — Brown hair and eyes, male, 40s.
- **Sierra** — Red hair, curly, green eyes, female, 6 years old.

## What the Party Knows

A family from Baldur's Gate traveling to Waterdeep for a better life. They made a deal with Regan Thomas — safe passage for a deferred loan — not knowing it was a trap. Penny and Thomas were kidnapped from their camp outside Nightstone. Sierra escaped on her own and was found hiding in the grain mill by Pip.

While Pip investigated, Sierra was looked after by Kaban Stone and Lefter Grinwald. The family was reunited that night.

### Session 7
Sierra ran to Pip the moment she saw her. The whole family thanked Pip and extended an open invitation to visit Penny's cousin, **Blossom Snobeedle**, at the Snobeedle Orchards and Meadery in Undercliff just outside Waterdeep. They were eager to help when the party asked about Regan — and not bothered in the slightest when assassination was implied. They didn't have much useful to add — their knowledge of him was vague.
