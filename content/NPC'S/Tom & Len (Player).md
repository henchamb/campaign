# Tom & Len
*Human · Male · Early 30s (both)*

**Appearance:** Baldurian, both with brown hair and eyes, roughly the same height (~5'10"). Easy to mistake for brothers at a glance.

## What the Party Knows

Members of the WSC 4th troupe, working in a support and covert capacity. Easy-going and fun-loving — but capable of being utterly ruthless when a fight demands it. They have a particular fondness for stealing from rich people.

### Pre-Session — Regan's Camp
Tom & Len were in Nightstone when Regan's trafficking operation was uncovered. They helped Pip rescue the victims from his camp and were the ones who handed Regan over to the Nightstone guard.

### Session 6 — The Gold
Ralli led the party to their camp. Tom & Len woke up slightly annoyed, recognized Pip, and handed the party a bag of Waterdeep Dragons. The party already knew the stolen Lord's Alliance payment had been in Waterdeep Dragons. The connection isn't hard to make. They also told the party the WSC was departing the next morning — and that the troupe was throwing a party that night to celebrate Ralli's return.

### Session 7 — Intel
The party came back with questions about Regan Thomas. Tom & Len knew: he was being held in the garrison on the southern island of Nightstone. They also knew that Pip had been the one to bring him in. They pointed the party toward the trafficking victims — the Ganders — for more detail.

### Session 8 — The Alibi
After the assassination, the party went straight to Tom & Len. Cover story: gambling with the WSC all night. They backed it. The party actually gambled — Esme took the pot.

### Session 9 — The Getaway
After the attack at the Boar, the party fled to the WSC camp before dawn. Tom & Len led them to their favorite hiding spot inside the performance tents. The caravan pulled out at 6am.

## Last Seen
WSC caravan, traveling toward Waterdeep with the party.
