# Lady Velrosa Nandar
*Human · Female*

## What the Party Knows
The lord and lawgiver of Nightstone. The party heard her name in their first moments in the village — they were told she was in charge. Her Keep sits on the southern island, connected by a bridge to the main square.

They have not met her. They've never asked about her.
