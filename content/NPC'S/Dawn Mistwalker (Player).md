# Dawn Mistwalker
*Wood Elf · Female*

**Appearance:** 5'7". Blonde hair, tan skin, green eyes. Carries herself with calm authority — the quietness of someone who has survived far longer than anyone around her.

---

## What the Party Knows

### First Contact — Sessions 1–2
Dawn was referred to the party by the Nightstone butcher's wife as a local game hunter who knew the goblin trails in the Ardeep Forest. When the party first knocked on her door, she cracked it open with mild suspicion — she has little warmth for Lord's Alliance business. When Marok appeared unexpectedly on her roof and the situation devolved, she shut the door entirely.

The party eventually reached her properly. She agreed to guide them to the goblin hideout along the game trail, but was clear: she would not help kill anything on behalf of the Lord's Alliance. Her real interest isn't hunting — it's the ancient history buried in the Ardeep Forest. She remembered her friendship with several of the last Moon Elves before their final departure in 1344 DR, roughly 150 years ago. She spoke of the forest as it once was: sprawling from the Golden Hills to the Sword Mountains, before human logging and agriculture reduced it to what remains. She holds the Lord's Alliance responsible for accelerating that destruction.

### Inside the Temple — Sessions 3–5
Marok, unknown to the rest of the party at the time, had a private encounter with Dawn near the temple entrance. She was suspicious of him but he convinced her to help. She had found a second entrance to the underground chambers.

During the temple raid, Dawn followed her curiosity inside. The sight of the Shar statues visibly unsettled her. She warned the party: the goblins might be the least of the dangers here. Despite persuasion, she still would not take a life for the Lord's Alliance — instead pressing a *Potion of Healing* into the party's hands before wishing them luck.

After the temple was cleared, Dawn returned to investigate the deepest chamber. She removed the mask from the corpse in the deepest chamber — revealing it to be **Frieda Greatheart**, Nightstone's constable. She allowed Esme to keep the mask, on one condition: they bring it to **Archmage Daneel** to be examined. She accompanied the party back to Nightstone by river, then traveled south with them to Daneel's tower.

### The Warning — Session 8
Dawn arrived at the Dreaming Boar with urgent news. She had spent time researching the mask in the temple's ancient library. Her findings: **the mask's enchantment cannot be broken by conventional dispelling.** Attempting to dispel it would only temporarily suppress its connection to Shar — the power would return. The mask was still dangerous.

Before the party could fully process this, they discovered Pip and the mask were gone.

### Revealed — Session 8
When the shadow demon overwhelmed the party in the main square, something hidden made itself known.

A giant dragon, beautiful with shining blue scales, descended on the square. A roar that shook the earth. A beam of light struck the shadow demon — and the party heard a voice echo inside their minds. **Dawn's voice.**

> *"Begone, foul creature. There is life here still and you are unwelcome. Return now to the void from whence you came."*

The demon, weakened, retreated — but took Pip underground with it.

In the aftermath, Dawn explained herself briefly: she is a **Spirit Dragon**, hatched from the ruins of the ancient Moon Elf civilization that once filled the Ardeep. Spirit Dragons grow into living embodiments of the lost place they came from — shaped by it, driven by it. The curiosity the party had witnessed in her all along — the obsession with the forest's history, the Moon Elves, the Baelnorn, the holy sites — wasn't scholarly interest. It *was* her. She is tethered to the Ardeep and cannot easily leave it.

She said nothing about Pip. The party was already moving — occupied with their own goals — and by the time they looked up, she was gone.

## Last Seen

Said farewell on the Trade Way as the WSC caravan departed Nightstone. Her direction from there is unknown, but the forest is her home.
