# Norm Amberway
*Halfling · Male*

**Appearance:** Sandy hair, rosy cheeks and nose. Constantly in motion — running food, pulling taps, managing some minor crisis — but genuinely cheerful about it. Speaks in a thick, distinctive accent.

## What the Party Knows

Owner and keeper of the Dreaming Boar in Nightstone. Norm runs a good inn. His wife Darcy cooks two meals a day — breakfast and supper — and the food is excellent. Travelers speak fondly of Darcy's cooking long after they've left. Their children help run the place and have developed sharp wits from handling unruly guests.

Norm keeps his ear out for gossip. Between travelers, merchants, and caravan folk passing through on the Trade Way, he hears a lot.

### Session 1
The party's home base. Vaama's connection with Norm got the group two free rooms on their first night.

### Session 6
The party came down to breakfast to find Darcy at the stove and Ralli passed out at the corner of the bar, drooling on the bartop, clutching a half-empty bottle of fortified wine. Norm said she'd been asking about the party all night. He offered to comp their breakfast and drinks if they took her back to her camp. Lent them his wagon.

### Session 8
Delivered a small wrapped package to the party — left in his keeping by Archmage Daneel.

### Session 9
Walked in on the aftermath of the fight in the party's room. Stunned, terrified, asking fumbling questions. Said he was going to summon the guard. The party dropped out the window before he could.

## Open Thread
Norm was left in that room with a dead body wearing Cassalantar cult medallions. The party was gone before it could catch up to them. What the guard made of it — and whether any of that follows the party to Waterdeep — is an open question.
