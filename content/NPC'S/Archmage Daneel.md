# Archmage Daneel
*Human · Male · Very old*

**Appearance:** Pale blue eyes. Long white hair and beard. Quick-tempered and deeply curious. Radiates power — do not mistake age for frailty.

## What the Party Knows

### Session 5 — The Tower
An immensely powerful wizard who has lived in a tower south of Nightstone for decades. The party brought the Sharran mask to him at Dawn's insistence. He examined it, announced he could dispel the ancient magic still tethered to it, and did so — or so the party believed.

When the party grew demanding, he responded by casting *Hold Person*, freezing all five of them simultaneously. He said his goodbye and left them standing there.

### Session 7 — The Square
Found standing atop the rubble pile in Nightstone's main square, studying the seismic event. He apologized for the Hold Person — acknowledged it was "unkind" to use such invasive magic against guests. He shared his analysis of what happened: a Sharran working, connected to the shadow weave, consistent with what the mask had already told him. He mentioned he might have single-use scrolls that could be useful and invited the party to drop by his tower.

### Session 8 — The Package
Norm handed the party a neatly wrapped package left in his keeping by Daneel. Inside: a scroll of Detect Thoughts. And a note, written in sweeping calligraphic script:

*"Had this scroll collecting dust in my library. It may offer you a chance at accomplishing your goal, whatever that may be. One need not compel the truth to be spoken to learn it. This spell, if successful, will grant you access to the thoughts of others. If you are tactful you may learn the truth even if your quarry is speaking lies.*

*P.S. Thank Dawn for this gift, she was very convincing on your behalf."*

**Note:** Dawn revealed later that same session that Daneel's work on the mask had only temporarily suppressed its connection to Shar — not severed it. The power returned. Pip had the mask when the shadow demon came.

## Last Seen
Nightstone main square — Session 7. Whereabouts otherwise unknown.
