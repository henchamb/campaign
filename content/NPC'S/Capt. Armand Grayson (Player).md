# Capt. Armand Grayson
*Human · Male · Late 30s*

**Appearance:** 6'2". Short blond hair, stubble, scars. Half-plate armor with captain's adornments. Professional and polite, but direct. Always stands with excellent posture.

## What the Party Knows

### Session 5
Briefly present in Godson's quarters when the party arrived to collect their receipt — on the receiving end of Godson's dressing-down over the stolen gold. Mermin stayed behind; Esme overheard the full conversation through their link.

### Session 6 — Looking for Frieda
Approached the party at the Dreaming Boar before breakfast. Thanked them for dealing with the goblins. Apologized for the stolen gold delay. Then, after a pause, got to his real reason: he was worried about Frieda. Described her recent behavior as odd and troubling. Said the party might have been the last people to speak with her before she disappeared — and asked if they knew anything.

The party played dumb. He took them at their word and left.

Later that session, the party agreed to go back and tell him the truth. The Ankheg came up through the rubble before they could. They left Nightstone without ever going back.

## Open Thread
Grayson doesn't know what happened to Frieda. He described her as his boss and friend. The party is the only ones who know the truth — and they haven't told him.

## Last Seen
Nightstone — managing the aftermath of the Ankheg attack and the obelisk incident.
