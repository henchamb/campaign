# Ralli Batterton
*Gnome · Female*

**Appearance:** Dark green hair with an undercut, purple eyes.

## What the Party Knows

### Session 4 — The Kitchen
Found hiding in the kitchen of the temple after the party cleared the dining hall. Small voice, cautious head poking through the door — then she introduced herself. She'd been taken captive by goblins roughly a week before the party arrived and put to work cooking for them.

Her feelings about goblins were made clear immediately. Before the party could stop her, she picked up an iron cooking pot and caved in the skull of their hogtied goblin hostage.

She knew two things that mattered: there were two hobgoblins still alive in the temple, and there was a figure called "the Lady" — someone the goblins feared deeply and paid tribute to, believing her to be a vessel of Shar. Ralli had never seen her. She helped narrow down where the hobgoblins were likely holed up.

Before leaving, she tore through a garbage pile until she found her instrument — the **Button Horn**. A brass instrument covered in buttons and valves, unlike anything the party had seen before. Her brother [[Nelvin Batterton]] built it for her. As far as anyone knows, it's the only one in the world.

### Session 5 — The Boat
Ralli stayed with the group for the river journey back to Nightstone. She was on the boat when Pip read Frieda's journal aloud — Undercommon sections included. She heard all of it.

Back in Nightstone, she vouched for the party before Godson Goodrich, confirming they'd completed the goblin contract.

### Session 6 — The Secret
Found the next morning passed out at the bar of the Dreaming Boar, drooling on the bartop, clutching a half-empty bottle of fortified wine. Norm mentioned she'd been asking about the party all night.

The party loaded her into Norm's wagon to return her to camp. She startled awake crossing the drawbridge, recognized them, and announced she had a secret. Led them — stumbling — back to the WSC camp and to the tent of Tom & Len, who were sleeping. She woke them roughly. The brothers gave the party a bag of gold as thanks for rescuing her — Waterdeep Dragons, not standard coin. The party already knew the stolen Lord's Alliance payment had been Waterdeep Dragons. The connection isn't hard to make.

## Last Seen
With the WSC caravan, heading toward Waterdeep alongside the party.
