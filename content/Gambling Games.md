# Gambling Games

A collection of games you may encounter in taverns, camps, and dark corners along the road. All are games of chance — though chance can be helped along by a skilled hand.

---

## The Crick Queen's Call
*A dice game. 3–6 players.*

**Setup:** Each player antes up and is dealt three dice (3d6), kept hidden.

**Play:** Players call, raise, or fold until all bets are even. A player who raises may be dealt one additional die — but it **replaces** one of their existing dice (their choice which).

**Showdown:** Players reveal. Best hand wins the pot.

**Hands (best to worst):**

| Hand | Example |
|---|---|
| Three of a Kind | 5, 5, 5 |
| Straight | 2, 3, 4 |
| Evens | 2, 4, 6 (only) |
| Odds | 1, 3, 5 (only) |
| Two of a Kind | 2, 2, 1 |
| High Roll | 6, 2, 1 |

Higher numbers beat lower in equivalent hands. (Three 5s beats Three 4s. Straight 4–5–6 beats Straight 3–4–5.)

## Baldur's Bones
*A dice game. 3+ players.*

**Setup:** Each player antes up. Highest roll goes first.

**Play:** Each player rolls three dice on their turn. Proceeding clockwise, players choose to **stand** or **roll**.
- **Stand:** Pass to the next player.
- **Roll:** Take one additional d6 and add it to your total. If your total exceeds **21**, you bust and are eliminated.

Players can keep rolling on their turn until they stand or bust.

**Showdown:** Highest total among players still in wins the pot.

## Greypeaks
*A dice game. 2+ players. Named for the three peaks of the Greypeak Mountains.*

**Setup:** Each player antes up.

**Round 1:** Each player secretly rolls 1d8 and keeps it hidden. Betting round — raise, call, or fold.

**Round 2:** Each player secretly rolls 1d6 and adds it to their hidden total. Betting round.

**Round 3:** Each player secretly rolls 1d4 and adds it to their hidden total. Final betting round.

**Showdown:** All players reveal. Highest combined total (d8 + d6 + d4) wins the pot.

## Copper Dragon
*Also called Silver Dragon, Gold Dragon, or Dragon Eyes. 4+ players.*

**Setup:** Each player starts with **two coins** (the denomination sets the stakes). Players sit in a circle.

**Play:** Players take turns rolling 2d6 clockwise.

| Roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| Any single 1 | Put 1 coin in the pot |
| Double 1 ("Dragon Eyes") | Put 2 coins in the pot |
| Any single 6 | Pass 1 coin to the player on your left |
| Double 6 | Pass 2 coins to the player on your left |
| Anything else | No effect |

A player with no coins skips their turn until a coin is passed to them.

**Endgame:** When only one player has coins remaining, they get **three throws** to avoid rolling any 6. Succeed — win the pot. Roll a 6 — pass coins and dice to the next player, who gets their own three throws. Continue until someone clears it.

## GUNDBARG!
*A dice game. 2–6 players. Named after the great port city of Gunderland.*

Uses the rules of **King of Tokyo**.

Players roll 6d6 and may re-roll any dice up to two times, locking in results they want to keep. Dice faces: **Claw** (deal 1 damage), **Heart** (heal 1 HP — only if you're outside the city), **Lightning** (score energy/points), **1**, **2**, **3** (score VP if you roll three or more of a kind).

One player holds **Gunderland** at a time — they collect 2 energy per turn and deal damage to all other players, but cannot heal. Everyone else attacks whoever holds Gunderland. Last player standing wins — or first to 20 VP.

---

> [!tip] Cheating
> A skilled hand can tip the odds. Any player can attempt to cheat — roll **Sleight of Hand** against the other players' **passive Perception**. Succeed and no one's the wiser. Fail and the table knows.
